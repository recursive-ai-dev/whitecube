Evidence for the defect:
- Installed-system journal, last boot: reassociation requests at +30/+45/+75 s after service
  start, then a locally generated disconnect (kernel: Reason 2 PREV_AUTH_NOT_VALID x3, then
  Reason 3 DEAUTH_LEAVING) and silence. Replaying the old decision functions
  (net/legacy/cube-net.v1) with every probe healthy and DHCP taking 19 s reproduces the
  bounce at +30 s and +45 s; the same timeline leaves the new Supervisor idle.
- dhcpcd.conf had no timeout: dhcpcd(8)/dhcpcd.conf(5) say a single-interface dhcpcd exits when
  it expires (default 30 s); DHCP finished 3 s before that deadline in the journal.

  no-lease timer from association start, flat backoff, 30 s grace, ignored dead router,
  dhcpcd timeout removed) are each caught; one initially survived, the test was strengthened.

  ip -j neigh (REACHABLE in 0 s; an unowned address FAILED in 3.6 s), UDP DNS to the router's
